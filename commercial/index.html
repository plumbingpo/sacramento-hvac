Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Sacramento HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan service around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Sacramento HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Sacramento and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms.
