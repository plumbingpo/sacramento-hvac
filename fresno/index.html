Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Fresno

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
